Method

A clear framework for moving swiftly without sacrificing precision.

The approach reduces noise, makes choices intelligible and installs an execution discipline suited to the pace of the business.

1

Diagnosis of the real situation

Read the business, its people and its constraints quickly and without preconception. Identify what is working, what is blocking and what is being avoided.

2

Identification of critical priorities

Separate urgent from important. Focus the organisation on the two or three levers that will create the most visible and durable movement.

3

Concise and measurable action plan

Translate priorities into an executable plan with clear owners, defined milestones and indicators that can be tracked without a dashboard committee.

4

Alignment of key teams

Make the direction and the logic behind decisions legible to every person who has to act on them. Resistance drops when people understand the reasoning.

5

Follow-up on decisions and outcomes

Hold the pace. Review progress regularly, correct course early and transfer methods to the organisation so the gains hold after the mission ends.
